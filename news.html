Colombo's Wetlands Find New Defenders in Stories, Histories and Heritage

May 8, 2026 | Heritage Community

A first-of-its-kind project is using storytelling, history and cultural heritage to build lasting community stewardship of Colombo's threatened urban wetlands. The two-year project, led by IWMI with the University of Hull and University of Kelaniya, was awarded through the British Academy's International Interdisciplinary Research Projects 2026 programme [citation:4]. Urban development and pollution have reduced the water storage capacity of Colombo's wetlands by 40% since 2001, even as the city faces intensifying flood risk [citation:4].

April 21, 2026 Research Collaboration

University of Hull to Lead Sri Lanka Wetlands Project

The University of Hull is leading a new two-year project supporting wetland management in Colombo's Kotte District. Researchers will explore how cultural heritage, memory and storytelling can inform wetland management, working with communities facing rapid development, pollution and climate pressures [citation:2].

February 2, 2026 Events Heritage

World Wetlands Day 2026: Celebrating Traditional Knowledge

World Wetlands Day 2026 was observed under the theme "Wetlands and Traditional Knowledge: Celebrating Cultural Heritage." The theme highlights the enduring role of indigenous and traditional knowledge systems in sustaining wetland ecosystems while preserving cultural identity [citation:3]. IWMI Director General Mark Smith emphasised that conservation succeeds when science and culture work hand in hand [citation:17].

April 17, 2026 Conservation Infrastructure

New Jetty to Protect Negombo Lagoon and Attract Tourists

Construction of a new jetty and boat launching yard at a cost of Rs. 318 million was inaugurated to conserve Negombo Lagoon. The project aims to restore order to boat mooring, preserve the lagoon's scenic beauty, and benefit both the fishing community and the tourism industry [citation:8].

July 26, 2026 Climate Community

Communities Lead Mangrove Restoration in Mullaitivu District

Communities in Mullaitivu District have planted over 24,000 mangrove saplings in Nayaru Lagoon, reaching nearly 50% of their target. The UN-Habitat supported initiative, funded by the Adaptation Fund, is restoring two hectares of degraded mangrove habitats while building local climate resilience [citation:10].

June 13, 2026 Research Biodiversity

First Integrated Assessment of Puttalam Lagoon Seagrass Meadows

A new study provides the first integrated socio-ecological assessment of seagrass meadows in Puttalam Lagoon, identifying five seagrass species and examining how monsoonal variability influences carbon storage. The research contributes to SDG 13 (Climate Action), SDG 14 (Life Below Water), and SDG 15 (Life on Land) [citation:6].

October 9-12, 2026 Events Research

Wellness in Wetlands 2026: 2nd International Conference in Sri Lanka

The Wellness in Wetlands 2026 conference will bring together researchers, policymakers, conservationists and public health professionals in Sri Lanka to explore how wetlands can support environmental sustainability and human wellbeing. Prof. Simon Bell from the University of Edinburgh will deliver a keynote address [citation:5].
